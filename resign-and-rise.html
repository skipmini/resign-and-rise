Plan your next move before you leave.

Resign & Rise is a personal transition planning tool for anyone weighing whether — and how — to leave their job. It's not here to tell you to quit. It's here to help you understand your readiness, prepare what's missing, and leave with a plan rather than a leap.

How it works

Start with a short readiness check across four areas: financial, career, well-being, and practical logistics. From there, you get a personalized checklist, a runway calculator, and a timeline — so whenever you do decide to move, you're ready for it.

What this isn't

This tool doesn't give financial advice, diagnose burnout, or make the decision for you. If your well-being answers suggest things feel heavier than usual, we'll say so plainly and point you toward getting proper support — separate from the general assessment.
